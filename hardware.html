How it works

GPS distance. Smart auto-start.

Mileage Matters measures every trip with GPS. Optional car Bluetooth pairing helps the app recognize your vehicle and auto-start tracking — it does not read an odometer or require any plug-in adapter.

What powers each trip

📍

GPS distance

Trip miles are derived from GPS location samples on your iPhone. That distance is what appears on trip cards and in IRS exports.

🏃

Core Motion

Apple's motion APIs help classify when you are driving so the app can wake and start a trip without you tapping Start.

🎧

Car Bluetooth

Pair your vehicle's stereo or hands-free Bluetooth once. When your phone connects to that device, Mileage Matters can recognize the configured vehicle and help auto-start.

Setup tips

1. Grant Location (Always) and Motion

Automatic detection needs Always location authorization so iOS can wake the app in the background. Motion access improves driving classification. You can change either later in iOS Settings.

2. Pair your car's Bluetooth (optional)

In the app, open your vehicle and pair the Bluetooth device your iPhone already uses for audio or calls — typically the car stereo or hands-free profile. Mileage Matters uses that connection for vehicle recognition and auto-start, not for reading odometer data.

3. Drive, then classify

Trips save with GPS-derived distance. Swipe or bulk-classify as Business, Medical, Charity, or Personal, then export CSV, PDF, or HTML when you need records for your CPA.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any hardware?

No. Your iPhone is enough. Car Bluetooth pairing is optional and uses the stereo or hands-free profile you already have — there is nothing extra to buy or plug in under the dash.

Is distance from the car's odometer?

No. Distance is GPS-derived only. Bluetooth is used only to recognize a configured vehicle and help start trips automatically.

Will GPS match my dashboard exactly?

Not always. GPS can differ from a mechanical or digital odometer by a small percentage because satellite samples don't capture every curve. That is expected for phone-based mileage tracking.

What if I don't pair Bluetooth?

You can still use automatic detection via GPS speed and Core Motion. Pairing Bluetooth simply helps the app know which vehicle you're in and improves auto-start reliability.

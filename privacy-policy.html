Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Mileage Matters ("the App") is a product of Ridgeline Digital LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information the App uses, how it is stored, and what (if anything) leaves your device.

Summary

  • Trip, route, and mileage data stay on your iPhone in Core Data.
  • We do not upload trip data to Ridgeline Digital LLC servers.
  • We do not show ads or sell your driving history.
  • Optional crash reports (Sentry) exclude trip, route, and location details.
  • Optional feedback you choose to send goes through Formspree.

Location

The App uses location services on your device to detect trips and calculate GPS-derived distance. Location data used for trip tracking is processed and stored on your device. We do not operate a Ridgeline backend that receives your trip routes or continuous location history.

Automatic background detection requires iOS "Always" location authorization. You can change this permission in iOS Settings at any time; doing so may limit automatic tracking.

Motion

The App uses Apple Core Motion (and related activity classification) to help detect when you are driving so trips can start and stop automatically. Motion signals are used on-device for detection; they are not uploaded to Ridgeline as trip analytics.

Bluetooth

If you choose to pair a vehicle, the App may use Bluetooth to recognize a configured vehicle Bluetooth device — typically your car's stereo or hands-free / calling profile. This helps with vehicle recognition and auto-start.

Bluetooth is not used to read odometer data from the vehicle. Distance in the App is GPS-derived only.

Trip data storage

Trip information (including times, GPS-derived distance, classifications, notes, and related records you enter) is stored locally using Core Data on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes this local data unless it has been preserved through your own exports or Apple services you control (such as iCloud backup, if enabled).

Maps and geocoding

The App uses Apple MapKit and Apple Geocoding services to display maps, routes, and human-readable addresses or nearby place names. Those requests are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Crash reporting (Sentry)

The App may send crash and diagnostic reports through Sentry to help us fix bugs. Crash reports are configured to avoid including personally identifiable trip content: we do not intentionally include trip lists, routes, GPS tracks, or mileage logs in those reports. Reports may include technical details such as device model, OS version, and stack traces.

Feedback (Formspree)

If you submit optional in-app or website feedback, that message (and any contact details you include) is delivered via Formspree so we can respond. Only send information you are comfortable sharing for support purposes.

iCloud

If you enable iCloud Backup (or other Apple sync features that include app data), Apple may store a copy of App data according to Apple's terms and privacy policy. That is controlled by your Apple ID settings, not by a Ridgeline cloud account.

What we do not do

  • No advertising networks in the App for trip data.
  • No analytics products that upload your trip history to Ridgeline.
  • No sale of your mileage or location history.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

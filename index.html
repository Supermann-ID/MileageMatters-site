Features

Built for the way you actually drive.

No spreadsheets. No forgetting to hit "start." Just drive, classify, and export.

🚗

Automatic trip detection

Drives are detected using GPS, Core Motion, and (optionally) your car's Bluetooth stereo or hands-free profile. Trips start and stop on their own — you just classify them.

📡

Car Bluetooth auto-start

Pair your vehicle's Bluetooth once. When your iPhone connects to that stereo or hands-free device, Mileage Matters can recognize the vehicle and help auto-start the trip. Distance is always GPS-derived.

📊

IRS-compliant reports

Export CSV for your accountant, a formatted PDF for your records, or an HTML dashboard for yourself. All the fields Publication 463 asks for.

🏷️

Quick classification

Business, medical, charity, or personal — one tap. Bulk classify connected trip legs, with a 30-second undo window for fat-finger mistakes.

📍

Routes & locations

Every trip shows on a map with its route, start and end points, and nearby businesses — so you know exactly where you went, months later. Powered by Apple Maps and geocoding.

🔒

Your data stays yours

All trip data lives on your device in Core Data. No ads, no trip analytics, no upload to Ridgeline servers. Crash reports (Sentry) never include trip or location data. Uninstall and your data goes with it.

How it works

Three steps to better records.

1

Install & grant permissions

Mileage Matters needs Location (Always) and Motion access to detect trips automatically. Optionally pair your car's Bluetooth for vehicle recognition. Permissions are explained up front.

2

Drive normally

Go about your day. The app wakes up when you start driving and logs GPS-derived distance in the background. No buttons to press.

3

Classify and export

When you're ready, swipe each trip as Business, Medical, Charity, or Personal. Export CSV, PDF, or HTML at tax time and send it straight to your CPA. Siri shortcuts help too.

Privacy first

Built so an audit starts on your phone — not in our cloud.

Mileage Matters stores trips on-device. We don't sell your driving history, run ads against it, or upload routes to Ridgeline Digital LLC servers. Optional feedback goes through Formspree only when you send it. Read the full policy anytime.

Features

Built for the way you actually drive.

No spreadsheets. No forgetting to hit "start." No guessing at the odometer reading six months later.

🚗

Automatic trip detection

Drives are detected using GPS, motion, and (optionally) your car's Bluetooth. Trips start and stop on their own — you just classify them.

⚡

OBD-II odometer capture

Plug in a compatible OBD-II adapter and Mileage Matters reads your car's real odometer at the start and end of each trip. IRS-grade accuracy.

📊

IRS-compliant reports

Export CSV for your accountant, a formatted PDF for your records, or a web dashboard for yourself. All the fields Publication 463 asks for.

🏷️

Quick classification

Business, medical, charity, or personal — one tap. A 30-second undo window keeps you from worrying about fat fingers.

📍

Routes & locations

Every trip shows on a map with its route, start and end points, and nearby businesses — so you know exactly where you went, months later.

🔒

Your data stays yours

All trip data lives on your device. No cloud upload, no analytics, no ads. Uninstall the app and your data goes with it.

How it works

Three steps to better records.

1

Install & grant permissions

Mileage Matters needs Location (Always) and Motion access to detect trips automatically. Permissions are explained up front and you can toggle any of them later.

2

Drive normally

Go about your day. The app wakes up when you start driving and logs the trip in the background. No buttons to press.

3

Classify and export

When you're ready, swipe each trip as Business, Medical, Charity, or Personal. Export a CSV or PDF at tax time and send it straight to your CPA.

Get more accurate

OBD-II adapters make every mile count.

GPS tracking is great — but it can miss short segments, parking lots, and the last few hundred feet home. A $20 OBD-II adapter reads your car's actual odometer so your mileage records match what the IRS will check against if they ever audit.
